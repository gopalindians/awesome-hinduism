### Agamas

- [Āgama (Hinduism)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agama_%28Hinduism%29) — Śaiva, Śākta, and Vaiṣṇava scriptural corpora. See also [Tantra](#tantra) for tantric śāstra and major online editions.

### Tantra

**Tantra** names a wide class of Hindu śāstras—ritual manuals, yogic discourse, and theology—closely related to the **Āgama** traditions (Śaiva, Śākta, and Vaiṣṇava, including Pāñcarātra). The links below support **text-historical study**; initiatory practice is traditionally transmitted through **qualified gurus** and living lineages, not from excerpts alone.

**Overviews and traditions**

- [Tantra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantra) — general article (multiple Asian contexts; Hindu sections within).
- [Tantras (Hinduism)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantras_%28Hinduism%29) — survey of Hindu tantric scripture types.
- [Śaivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaivism) — Śiva-centered śāstras and schools, including many Śaiva Āgamas and Tantras.
- [Śāktism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaktism) — Devī-centered tantric and non-tantric traditions.
- [Kaula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaula) — Kula / Kaula ritual and social context in Śākta–Śaiva discourse.
- [Kashmir Śaivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir_Shaivism) — Pratyabhijñā and related non-dual Śaiva systems.
- [Trika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trika) — threefold schema central to Kashmiri Śaiva praxis and exegesis.
- [Abhinavagupta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhinavagupta) — philosopher–exegete; author of the *Tantrāloka*.
- [Pāñcarātra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pancharatra) — Vaiṣṇava āgamic corpus; includes tantra-style śāstras such as the *Lakṣmī Tantra*.
- [Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vij%C3%B1%C4%81na_Bhairava_Tantra) — short Śaiva yoga–meditation text, widely commented in modern times.

**Major texts and studies (digital)**

- [Tantrāloka of Abhinavagupta (Sanskrit)](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/tantraloka-sanskrit-text) — encyclopedic Kashmiri Śaiva treatise (10th c.).
- [Svacchandatantra (Sanskrit)](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/svacchanda-tantra-sanskrit) — major Śaiva ritual śāstra associated with Svacchanda Bhairava.
- [Kubjikāmatatantra (Sanskrit)](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/kubjikamatatantra-sanskrit) — Kaula śāstra focused on goddess Kubjikā.
- [Lakṣmī Tantra (Sanskrit)](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/lakshmi-tantra-sanskrit) — Pāñcarātra work centered on Śrī/Lakṣmī.
- [Īśānaśivagurudevapaddhati (English study)](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/essay/isanasivagurudeva-paddhati-study) — 11th-c. paddhati; includes a long survey chapter on tantric literature.
- [Kulārṇavatantra (concept article)](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept/kularnavatantra) — influential Kaula śāstra; significance and citations on Wisdom Library.
- [Kulārṇava Tantra (scan, Motilal Banarsidass)](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.545875) — digitized print edition (Sanskrit text with English readings).
- [Tantric Texts series (Arthur Avalon / Woodroffe era)](https://archive.org/details/Tantric_Texts_Series_Edited_by_Arthur_Avalon_John_Woodroffe) — collected early 20th-c. editions of several tantras and related works.

## Deities and sacred geography

### Trimurti and related traditions

Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Śiva (Maheśa) are the classical Purāṇic triad; related figures and places below are introductory only.

- [Trimūrti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trimurti) — triad of creation, preservation, and dissolution in many Purāṇic and smṛti schemes.
- [Brahmā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahma) — creator figure in the classical triad; theology and iconography vary by text and region.
- [Viṣṇu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishnu) — preserver deity; avatāra doctrine is central in Vaiṣṇava traditions.
- **Śiva (Maheśa)** — [Lord Śiva and His Worship](http://www.dlshq.org/download/lordsiva.htm) — free book from Divine Life Society.
  - **Family (common Purāṇic lists):** Satī, Pārvatī, Gaṇeśa, Kārttikeya.
  - **Places:** [Amarnath (map)](https://goo.gl/maps/8nfCDRA2T381xgCE8); [Kedarnath Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kedarnath_Temple); [Tuṅganāth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungnath).
- [Gaṇeśa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganesha) — widely venerated as remover of obstacles; son of Śiva and Pārvatī in common Purāṇic lists.
- [Kārttikeya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kartikeya) — war god and son of Śiva; Skanda/Kumāra names and regional emphasis vary.
- [Hanuman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanuman) — Rāma’s monkey-general in the Rāmāyaṇa; enduring bhakti figure across traditions.
- [Char Dham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Char_Dham) — four major Himalayan pilgrimage centers; which four are counted differs by region and tradition.

### Goddesses

- [Devī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devi) — overview of the goddess in Hindu thought and practice.
- [Durgā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga) — warrior goddess; central to Śākta and broader festival worship (e.g. Navarātri).
- [Kālī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kali) — fierce form of the Goddess; widely discussed in Bengali and other Śākta contexts.
- [Lakṣmī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakshmi) — prosperity and śrī; paired with Viṣṇu in many Vaiṣṇava narratives.
- [Pārvatī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parvati) — Śiva’s consort; gentle counterpole to fierce Devī forms in many stories.
- [Sarasvatī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraswati) — knowledge, speech, and arts; revered across śāstric and popular settings.
- [Vaishno Devī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaishno_Devi) — Jammu hill shrine and major North Indian pilgrimage.
- [Bhadrakālī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhadrakali) — fierce Kālī-related form; strong presence in Kerala and other regional traditions.

### Mathas

- [Jyotir Maṭh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyotir_Math) — northern cardinal maṭha near Joshimath, Uttarakhand; linked in tradition to Ādi Śaṅkara’s Amnaya network.
- [Dvārakā Śāradā Pīṭhaṃ (Dwarka Sharada Peetham)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwarka_Sharada_Peetham) — western cardinal maṭha at Dwārakā, Gujarat; Paścimāmnāya seat in the same broad scheme.
- [Govardhan Maṭha, Puri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Govardhan_Math) — eastern cardinal maṭha at Purī; one of the four Amnaya maṭhas associated with Ādi Śaṅkara’s tradition (official `govardhanpeeth.org` may block automated checks).
- [Śṛṅgeri Śāradā Pīṭhaṃ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sringeri_Sharada_Peetham) — southern cardinal maṭha at Śṛṅgeri, Karnataka; Dakṣiṇāmnāya seat in the same broad scheme.
- [Kāñci Kāmakoṭi Pīṭham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanchi_Kamakoti_Peetham) — major Advaita pīṭha at Kāñcīpuram; scholarly and popular influence are large, while its dating relative to the four cardinal maṭhas is debated.

- **[Rāmānandī Sampradāya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanandi_Sampradaya)** — Vaiṣṇava bhakti lineage; [Rāmānanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramananda).
- **Akhāṛās (orders at Kumbh and related gatherings)** — [Akhāṛā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhara); [Akhil Bharatiya Akhara Parishad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhil_Bharatiya_Akhara_Parishad); [Niranjani Akhara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niranjani_Akhara); [Daśanāmi Sampradāya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashanami_Sampradaya); background in [Kumbh Mela § Akharas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumbh_Mela#Akharas:_Warrior_monks,_recruitment_drive_and_logistics).
